## Overview

This repository contains the research materials, literature review, comparative analyses, figures, tables, and manuscript developed for the review paper:

**"The Impact of Genetic Engineering on Human Diseases: Advances in Gene Therapy, Genome Editing, and Precision Medicine."**

The review explores how genetic engineering has transformed the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of human diseases. It discusses the molecular basis of genetic engineering technologies, including recombinant DNA technology, CRISPR-Cas9, RNA interference (RNAi), gene therapy, stem cell engineering, and precision medicine. The review also examines current clinical applications, ethical concerns, regulatory frameworks, and future prospects of genetic engineering in modern healthcare.

---

# Objectives

- Review the principles of genetic engineering in medicine.
- Explain the molecular mechanisms underlying genetic engineering technologies.
- Discuss the role of gene therapy in treating inherited and acquired diseases.
- Compare modern genome editing techniques including CRISPR-Cas9.
- Evaluate the impact of genetic engineering on personalized medicine.
- Examine ethical, legal, and regulatory challenges.
- Highlight future research directions in human disease treatment.
